Frequently Asked Questions

What is AfghaNutri?
AfghaNutri is a nutrition tracking platform designed to help users monitor calories, track progress, and build healthier habits while enjoying traditional Afghan foods.
Can I set my own health goals?
Yes. You can create personalized goals for weight loss, weight gain, or weight maintenance.
How are calories calculated?
Standardized Afghan recipes are used, then divided into servings to estimate per-plate calories.
Can I track physical activities?
Yes. Activities such as walking, running, and swimming can be logged to estimate calories burned.
Can I monitor my progress over time?
Yes. AfghaNutri provides weekly, monthly, and overall progress tracking for calories, activities, and weight changes.
Are the nutrition values exact?
Values are estimates and may vary based on cooking methods and ingredients.
